# Materials Today Bio microscopy panels

Author-provided panel excerpts from David R. Maestas Jr. et al.,
"Resolving differential vascular graft remodeling using longitudinal multiphoton
tracking in a 3D culture platform," Materials Today Bio 40 (2026), 103626.
https://doi.org/10.1016/j.mtbio.2026.103626

- Figure 4g: 8-week cultured vascular graft, 16x objective; 100 micrometer scale bar.
- Figure 4a: longitudinal culture imaging from 2 to 8 weeks; original channel labels and 100 micrometer scale bars.
- Figure 4e: 3D reconstruction of a 6-week culture with Hoechst-stained nuclei; no scale bar appears in the original panel.

The complete selected panels retain the original labels, channel keys and scale
bars. No color, contrast, opacity, geometry or content changes were made beyond
isolating the panels from the author-supplied Figure 4. The PNG crops match the
source pixels, and the WebP copies are pixel-identical lossless encodings. PNG
files provide full-resolution panel views. The original panel-a channel-label
typography is preserved; its apparent bandwidth typo is not repeated in captions.
