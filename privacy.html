Privacy

Your analytics choice

This site uses optional analytics only after you choose to allow them. Analytics are used to understand how visitors find and use the academic content, not for advertising.

Review privacy choices

Information analytics may collect

If you allow analytics, Google Analytics 4 may record the page viewed, referral source, device or browser category, scroll engagement, outbound-link interactions, publication-link interactions, CV PDF clicks or download attempts, and contact-method interactions.

GA4 may also provide approximate country, region, or city information. This site does not intentionally collect precise GPS location.

How the information is used

Analytics help identify which pages, publications, and resources are useful to visitors. No advertising, remarketing, fingerprinting, heatmaps, or session recording are used.

No form contents are intentionally sent. Email addresses are not intentionally included in custom analytics events.

Your choice

You can decline analytics, and you can change your choice later by selecting Privacy choices in any page footer. Revoking consent prevents analytics from initializing on subsequent page loads and removes accessible first-party Google Analytics cookies where technically possible. Your choice is saved in browser local storage so it can be remembered between pages. Your choice is saved in browser local storage so it can be remembered between pages.

Important limitations

A CV download event means that the PDF link was activated; it does not prove the file was ultimately saved or read. Analytics totals are incomplete by nature because consent choices, browser restrictions, blockers, and other technical limitations can prevent collection. Geographic information is approximate.
